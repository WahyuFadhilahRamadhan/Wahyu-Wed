Referensi

    
    
      
  • AIAG, Measurement Systems Analysis (MSA) Reference Manual, 4th ed. — sumber utama metode Average & Range dan ANOVA, konstanta K1/K2/K3 (d2, d2*), serta kriteria %GRR (<10% diterima, 10–30% bersyarat, >30% ditolak) dan ndc (≥5 kategori).
    • 
      
  • Aturan pooling interaksi Operator×Part: dilakukan otomatis jika p-value interaksi > 0.25, sesuai rekomendasi AIAG/Minitab.
    • 
      
  • ndc (number of distinct categories) = ⌊1.41 × PV / GRR⌋, berdasarkan Wheeler's Discrimination Ratio.
